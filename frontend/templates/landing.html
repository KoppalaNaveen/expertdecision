About EDRP

Expert Decision Replay Platform

Empowering modern enterprises with governance, clarity, and institutional decision intelligence.

Project Description

The Expert Decision Replay Platform (EDRP) is a centralized enterprise decision management system that enables organizations to create, review, approve, track, collaborate, and archive important business decisions while maintaining complete transparency, governance, auditability, and institutional knowledge. EDRP ensures critical business choices are documented with their surrounding context, meeting notes, and trade-offs, preventing organizational memory loss.

Mission

Empower organizations to make, document, and replay high-impact decisions with complete transparency, accountability, and seamless team collaboration.

Vision

To become the global enterprise standard for decision management, institutional knowledge retention, and compliance governance across modern workplaces.

Objectives

Streamline multi-tiered approval chains, preserve business rationale, eliminate approval bottlenecks, and provide full audit readiness for compliance.

Key Benefits

Enhanced organizational transparency, faster decision turnaround times, reduced operational risk, and easy historical decision replay for onboarding and review.

Privacy Policy
1. Information We Collect

We collect essential user profile details (such as full name, employee ID, and corporate email), role permissions, decision records, meeting notes, comments, and audit log telemetry necessary to operate the EDRP platform safely and efficiently.

2. How We Use Data

Collected information is used exclusively to process organizational workflows, manage approval chains, deliver system notifications, maintain compliance audit trails, and enforce security controls.

3. User Privacy & Confidentiality

EDRP adheres to strict enterprise data confidentiality standards. Your organizational decision data is protected and will never be shared, sold, or disclosed to unauthorized external entities.

4. Cookies & Local Session State

We utilize essential session cookies and local authentication tokens strictly required for verifying user identity, managing session lifetimes, and retaining user interface preferences.

5. Data Security

All network data transmissions are protected using standard TLS/SSL encryption. Data stored within our backend systems is secured using role-based access control and database isolation.

6. Data Retention

Decision records, version histories, and audit logs are retained in accordance with your organization's internal compliance policies and regulatory retention requirements.

7. User Rights

Users have the right to inspect their account profile, review assigned roles and permissions, and request corrections to personal profile attributes through platform administrators.

8. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries or compliance questions, please contact our data governance officer at privacy@edrp-platform.com.
