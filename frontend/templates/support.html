Navigate to 'Create Decision' in the sidebar. Fill in the title, problem statement, evaluated alternatives (with cost/risk metrics), and submit for approval.

Go to 'My Decisions' and filter by 'In Review'. You can click on any decision to see reviewer progress and timeline notes.

Once submitted, decisions are locked from direct editing. If a reviewer requests changes, status returns to Draft, allowing you to update and create version v2.

EDRP supports PDF, Word documents (.docx), PowerPoint presentations (.pptx), and CSV files up to 200MB in size.
